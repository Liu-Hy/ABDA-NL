Last updated September 4, 2026

ABDA-NL is a research prototype for exploring assumption-based defeasible argumentation. By using the public service, you agree to use it responsibly and in accordance with these terms.

Research use and limitations

The service is provided for research, education, and demonstration. It is not legal, medical, financial, safety-critical, or other professional decision support. Model-generated explanations and edit proposals can be incomplete or wrong. Review every proposal before applying it.

The ABDA engine, rather than the language model, computes the displayed argument graph and conclusion labels for the supplied scenario. That separation does not establish that a user-authored scenario accurately represents the real world.

Private project content, model prompts, and model responses are not analyzed as research data.
